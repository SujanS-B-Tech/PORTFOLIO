AGRONEON AI

Explainable AI for Smart Farming

AGRONEON AI is a smart agriculture platform focused on making Artificial Intelligence more accessible and useful for farmers. The platform combines agricultural data, AI-driven recommendations, market information, disease diagnostics, farm economics, and conversational assistance into a unified system.

Instead of providing recommendations without context, the platform focuses on explainable recommendations that help farmers understand why a particular action or crop decision is suggested. The system includes crop advisory features, agricultural economics calculations, disease diagnosis assistance, market intelligence, and an AI voice/chat assistant.

Key Features

  • Explainable AI-based crop recommendations & agricultural advisory
  • Disease diagnosis assistance & market intelligence
  • Farm economics, profit estimation, seed/fertilizer/labour calculations
  • AI voice/chat assistant with personalized insights
  • Secure authentication & persistent PostgreSQL database backend

Tech Stack

Frontend: React.js, modern web tech
Backend: Python, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy ORM
Database: PostgreSQL
Auth: JWT

Human Resource Management System

DayFlow is a centralized HRMS developed rapidly during the Odoo x NMIT Bangalore Hackathon 2026. It brings employee management, attendance tracking, leave applications, payroll, notifications, and administrative dashboards into a single, unified digital platform.

SEVASAKSHI

Smart NGO Inspection & Monitoring Platform

SEVASAKSHI is an AI-powered mobile and web platform designed to digitize NGO inspection for the Department of Social Justice & Empowerment (DoSJE). It replaces traditional paper-based auditing with a mobile, evidence-driven, geo-tagged digital workflow.

Field inspectors can utilize the offline-first app to capture timestamped photos, fill dynamic checklists, and sync data when networking restores. Crucially, an AI verification layer running YOLOv8 checks face liveness, duplication, and anomalies against submissions.

Key Features

  • Offline-first mobile operation & automated sync workflows
  • Geo-tagged, timestamped photo evidence verification
  • AI processing: Face liveness, duplicate detection, anomaly flagging
  • Centralized PMU web dashboard for audit tracking & analytics

Tech Stack

Mobile: Flutter
Web: React.js, Tailwind CSS
Backend: FastAPI, PostgreSQL, PostGIS, Redis
AI/ML: YOLOv8, OpenCV, TensorFlow Lite
Security: AES-256, TLS 1.3, JWT

Public Safety / SIH26231

SATYARANG

Digital Companion for Field Drug Testing

SATYARANG is an AI-assisted digital platform designed for the Narcotics Control Bureau. It aids field officers in conducting presumptive drug testing by using smartphone cameras to perform consistent color-reagent analysis regardless of variable lighting conditions.

The system uses HSV color-space normalization mapped to a predefined reaction matrix to identify substances and assign confidence scores. Crucially, each test produces a secure digital evidence certificate utilizing SHA-256 hashing to guarantee a tamper-evident chain of custody directly from the field.

Key Features

  • Offline-first smartphone presumptive color-reaction analysis
  • HSV-based image normalization and classification scoring
  • SHA-256 tamper-evident digital evidence certificate generation
  • GPS, timestamp, and officer identification logging

Tech Stack

Frontend: HTML5, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript (ES6+)
Backend: Node.js, Express.js
AI / Security: HSV Classification, Crypto-JS SHA-256
